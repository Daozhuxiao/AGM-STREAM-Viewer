# AGM STREAM Viewer Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 26, 2026**

This policy explains how AGM STREAM Viewer handles information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad.

## Information We Handle

The app requires no account. It processes device connection information, live video, photos, and recordings locally to connect to compatible hardware, display video, and save, download, edit, or manage media.

The app does not automatically upload this information to our servers or provide cloud storage. It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs that transmit user data. We do not sell your personal information or track you across apps or websites.

## Permissions

- **Local Network:** Connect to compatible hardware, view live video, and access device files.
- **Photos:** Save photos and videos to your Photos library at your request.

You can change permissions in system Settings at any time. Denying access may limit the related features.

## Sharing and Support

When you choose to share a photo, it is sent to your selected app or recipient. Their privacy practices apply. Device backups and Photos syncing depend on your Apple settings.

If you email support, Han receives your email address, message, and any attachments through QQ Mail. We use this information to respond to and resolve your request.

## Retention and Deletion

You can delete locally saved media in the app. Copies on your hardware device, in Photos, in backups, or held by recipients must be managed separately.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided.
