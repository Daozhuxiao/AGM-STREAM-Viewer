# AGM STREAM Viewer Support

AGM STREAM Viewer connects your iPhone or iPad to compatible devices over Wi-Fi for live video viewing, screenshots, recording, and photo and video management.

## Getting Started

1. Turn on your compatible device and connect your iPhone or iPad to its Wi-Fi network.
2. Open AGM STREAM Viewer and add the device to view live video.
3. Capture screenshots, record video, or browse and download photos and videos stored on the device.

Device connection, live viewing, and access to device files require compatible hardware and a connection to its Wi-Fi network.

## Troubleshooting

- **Cannot connect or view live video:** Confirm that the device is powered on and your phone or tablet is connected to the correct Wi-Fi network. Allow Local Network access if prompted, then reconnect and reopen the app.
- **Cannot save to Photos:** Check the app's Photos permissions and ensure your phone or tablet has enough available storage.
